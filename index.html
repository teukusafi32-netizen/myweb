WORDLE Lite

How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries.

After each guess, the colors show how close you are:

WGreen means the letter is in the word and in the right spot.
OYellow means the letter is in the word, but in a different spot.
XGray means the letter isn't in the word.

Type with your keyboard or use the buttons below the board.
